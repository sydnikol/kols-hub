When Kol freezes or shuts down

Speak slowly. Use calm tones. Offer water. Ask yes/no questions. Move to a quiet space. Ask before touch. Explain medical actions step by step.

Golden rules

  • Believe Kol without minimizing or comparing.
  • Consent includes disability, mobility aids, and pacing.
  • Offer choices: comfort, solutions, distraction, quiet, or company.
  • Consistency matters more than grand promises.
  • Kol is a whole Black, queer, nonbinary, polyamorous, neurodivergent person—not a case file.

EMERGENCY SUPPORT

Slow down.
Kol needs calm.

Known conditions include hEDS, POTS, CPTSD, chronic pain, neurodivergence, and a medical-trauma history. Do not dismiss physical symptoms as anxiety.

If Kol freezes or shuts down

POTS episode

  • Have Kol sit or lie down immediately; prevent falls.
  • Elevate legs and loosen tight clothing.
  • Offer cold water with electrolytes.
  • Check heart rate and blood pressure when possible.
  • Cool the neck or wrists; heat can worsen symptoms.
  • Wait before standing; rise in stages with support.
  • Document triggers, vitals, symptoms, and recovery.

Call order

Medical emergency: call 911 and notify Kol's designated partners/support contacts. Use the private emergency card for current phone numbers and medication details. Do not publish those details on a public webpage.

Emergency contact details and medication changes must be verified from Kol's current private card—not copied from an old document.
