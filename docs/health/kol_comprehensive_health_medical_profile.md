## POTS Episode Identification
### Symptoms
- Sudden dizziness
- Tachycardia (+30 bpm on standing)
- Visual changes (static, halos, blind spots)
- Pallor
- Slurred speech
- Weakness / collapse

### Immediate Care Protocol
1. Sit or lie down immediately
2. Elevate legs
3. Hydration with electrolytes
4. Cool environment
5. Monitor vitals
6. Gradual re-standing
